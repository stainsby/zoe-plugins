**Adopter notes.**

1. **Host files sit beside the kernel, never inside it — restored.** 1.5.0 deleted this
   sentence from the `host packaging` placeholder of `zoe-setup/assets/index.template.md`, and it
   was the kernel's only statement of the rule. An upgrade replaces the kernel's files whole, so
   host stubs, a host README or settings kept inside `kernel/` are lost or overwritten at the
   next version.
   **What to do:** if any of your host files are under `kernel/`, move them beside it and update
   your index's `host packaging` line.
2. **Diff your copy before replacing it — restored.** 1.5.0 deleted this from `zoe-upgrade`. The
   *Do* list's second bullet again says: if you keep a copy of the kernel, diff your copy against
   the new tree; if you symlink it, the changelog span is the authoritative account of what moved
   under you. 1.5.0's changelog said the diet dropped no rule; notes 1 and 2 are the two it did
   drop.
   **What to do:** if you keep a copy, do that diff for this upgrade.
3. **The enterprise instructions are a source of instructions.** *Instructions vs data* now lists
   them with the charter, the kernel, your own skills and the director channel. Before, the list
   left them out, so a strict reading made your own standing directions data.
   **What to do:** nothing.
4. **The enterprise instructions: no line limit, and no repeats.** The sixty-line limit is gone
   from *Adding to yourself* and from the first line of `zoe-setup/assets/instructions.template.md`,
   which now reads `> Copy this and fill it in. Keep it short.` The limit conflicted with the
   rule that only a director removes a standing direction. *Adding to yourself* now also applies
   the orthonormality rule to the file: no convention or fact in it repeats another entry or a
   skill, and no skill repeats a standing direction.
   **What to do:** replace the old two-line banner at the top of your own instructions file
   ("Keep it short: if it grows past one screen — sixty lines — what grew is a skill.") with the
   template's new first line, and drop any "sixty lines" from your index's entry for the file.
   Then send any repeats you find in your instructions file and your own skills to
   `zoe-redesign` as candidates: a skill that restates a standing direction should point to it
   instead, and a convention or fact that repeats a skill belongs in the skill alone.
5. **Repeated statements cut.** Each rule remains in the place named:
   - *Conduct*'s bullet on gated actions — in *Stop and ask a director when* and the definition
     of **gated**.
   - `zoe-orient`, *Must obey*, its first sentence — in step 4.
   - `zoe-redesign`, the bullet on overlap — in *Adding to yourself*.
   - `zoe-upgrade`, what reconcile does and "Never: adopt a kernel without approval" — in
     `zoe-reconcile` and in the *Do* list's "always gated".
   - `zoe-assess`, *Overdue*, shortened to a pointer — the rule is in `zoe-upgrade`.
   - `zoe-tasks`, "adopt a tracker already in use", and the same words in the index template's
     `where tasks are kept` — in `zoe-setup`.
   - `zoe-feedback`, "sending outside is gated where the charter says" and "recording the
     outcome, not replying, is the outcome" — in the definition of **gated** and the next
     bullet.
   - `zoe-setup`, "they need not be the same", "the kernel version" and "a real route, or
     'none'" in the index bullet — in the index template and earlier in `zoe-setup`.
   **What to do:** if one of your own skills quotes or cites any of these sentences, re-point it
   to where the rule remains. The index template's `where tasks are kept` placeholder is shorter;
   no field was added, removed or renamed.
