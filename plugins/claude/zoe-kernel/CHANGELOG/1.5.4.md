**Adopter notes.**

1. **Directors are quoted only if they choose to be.** `zoe-setup` now asks whether records
   should quote a director's words exactly or only say what they decided or asked; not quoting
   is the default. Where there are several directors, a quote shares each one's exact words with
   the rest. The answer goes in a new index line, `quote directors`, after `directors`.
   *Adding to yourself* now logs a standing direction with who gave it and when, in the
   director's own words only where that line says so. In
   `zoe-setup/assets/instructions.template.md`, "who gave it, when, and in what words is in the
   log" becomes "who gave it and when is in the log". This replaces the part of 1.5.2's note 1
   that asked for each standing direction to be logged in the director's own words.
   **What to do:** ask your director whether records should quote them, and add
   `quote directors:` to your index with the answer — "no" unless they choose otherwise. If your
   span also crosses 1.5.2, ask this first: where the answer is "no", the log entries 1.5.2's
   note 1 asks for record who gave each direction and when, not their words. Change that
   sentence under *Standing directions from directors* in your enterprise instructions to the
   new wording, whatever the answer. If "no", quote no director from now on, and send any of
   your own skills that ask for quotes to `zoe-redesign` as candidates to reword. Leave words
   already quoted in your records as they are; the log is never edited.
2. **A plainer phrase in *Verification*.** "Where the question is one of meaning" is now "Where
   the question needs judgement".
   **What to do:** nothing.
